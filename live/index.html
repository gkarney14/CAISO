Glossary

CAISO
California Independent System Operator. Runs the grid and wholesale power markets for most of California.
OASIS
Open Access Same-time Information System. CAISO's public market-data site and API, the source for every price on this page.
LMP
Locational Marginal Price. The price of one more MWh delivered at a specific point on the grid, in $/MWh. LMP = energy + congestion + losses + GHG.
MCE
Marginal Cost of Energy. The system-wide energy component of the LMP; it is the same at every location in an interval.
MCC
Marginal Cost of Congestion. The part of the LMP caused by transmission limits. Strongly negative values mean the area has more generation than the grid can export.
MCL
Marginal Cost of Losses. The part of the LMP that reflects electrical losses delivering power to or from the location.
MGHG
Greenhouse-gas component. Non-zero only for imports into California from the Western Energy Imbalance Market; normally $0 at Scarlet.
Pnode / APnode
Pricing node / aggregated pricing node. The point where CAISO calculates an LMP. Each resource settles at its own pnode, such as SCARLT_7_N011.
Trading hub (NP15, ZP26, SP15)
Aggregated price points for Northern (NP15), Central (ZP26) and Southern (SP15) California. Contracts and PPAs often settle at a hub.
Basis
Node price minus hub price. Negative basis means power at the project is worth less than at the hub, almost always because of congestion.
Cohort
On this page, the Scarlet I, II, III and Sonrisa pnodes. They clear within about $0.15/MWh of each other, so one node represents all of them.
DA / DAM
Day-Ahead Market. Hourly prices for tomorrow, cleared and published around 1 to 2 pm Pacific. Used here as the price forecast.
RT / RTM
Real-Time Market. 5-minute dispatch prices that settle actual deviations from the day-ahead schedule.
RTPD / FMM
Real-Time Pre-Dispatch, also called the Fifteen-Minute Market. 15-minute real-time prices, used in the simulator's price history.
HE
Hour Ending. HE1 is 00:00 to 01:00 Pacific, HE24 is 23:00 to 24:00. Fall-back day has a repeated HE2; spring-forward day has no HE3.
PT
Pacific Time, the time zone CAISO operates in (PST UTC−8 in winter, PDT UTC−7 in summer).
Negative price
An LMP below $0. Generators pay to inject; a battery is paid to charge. Common at Scarlet in solar hours (about HE10 to HE15).
Duck curve
The daily price shape on solar-heavy grids: low or negative at midday, high in the morning and evening ramps. It is what battery arbitrage captures.
BESS
Battery Energy Storage System.
MW / MWh
Power (megawatts) and energy (megawatt-hours). A 78 MW, 4-hour battery stores 312 MWh.
Arbitrage
Charging when power is cheap and discharging when it is expensive. Revenue = discharge price × MWh out − charge price × MWh in.
RTE
Round-trip efficiency. Share of charged energy returned on discharge; 85% means 100 MWh in, 85 MWh out.
Cycle
One full charge and discharge of the battery's energy capacity.
Degradation cost
An allowance in $/MWh discharged for battery wear, deducted in the simulation.
Perfect foresight
Simulation that knows every real-time price of the day in advance. The theoretical ceiling for arbitrage revenue.
DA schedule
Simulation that picks charge and discharge hours from day-ahead prices and is paid day-ahead prices.
DA plan, RT settle
Same day-ahead schedule, paid real-time prices instead. The gap to "DA schedule" shows the cost of forecast error.
Δ (delta)
In the forecast table, real-time actual minus day-ahead forecast for that hour.
$/kW-month
Revenue normalized by battery power rating per month; used to compare projects of different sizes.
RA
Resource Adequacy. Capacity payments for being available at peak; not included in these arbitrage estimates.
AS
Ancillary Services (regulation, spinning and non-spinning reserve). Separate revenue streams; not included here.
PPA
Power Purchase Agreement. Long-term contract for a project's output, often priced at a hub, which creates basis exposure.
